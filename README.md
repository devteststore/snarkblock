# Snark to Bricks

Turn your zkSNARK into a brick bust you can really build.

<h3 align="center">👉 <a href="https://devteststore.github.io/snarkblock/">Open Snark to Bricks</a> 👈</h3>
<p align="center">Free · runs in your browser</p>

## How to use

1. **Type your zkSNARK number** (1 to 10,000) and press **Build**. No number at hand? Click one of the examples. You can see your zkSNARK on [zilkroad.com](https://zilkroad.com).
2. **① Your bust**: watch it build in 3D, pick **Mini** (1 pixel = 1 stud) or **XL** (1 pixel = 2×2 studs), and turn it with your finger or mouse. Download a video of the build (square or 9:16).
3. **② Instructions**: flip through the step-by-step booklet on the page, then download it as a PDF or as a full kit (PDF + parts list).
4. **③ Buy the bricks**: see your shopping list, then **Buy at LEGO** (a Pick a Brick upload file) or **Buy on BrickLink** (a wanted list: Want → Upload → "Upload BrickLink XML format"). Turn on "Get every lot at LEGO" to rebuild the bust with parts LEGO sells.

Nothing is sold here: you order and pay on LEGO or BrickLink. Always check your cart before paying.
